# Retired programme

This directory holds the complete published record of the four-series programme that ran
from 2026-09-10 to 2026-09-21. It was retired in favour of a two-series
programme. Nothing published here was withdrawn, edited or recalculated: these are the
same files, under the same hashes, moved so the active programme's ledgers are not
confused with a closed one.

Its commitments remain under `commitments/programme-02/` and are still checked by
`scripts/verify.py` against the registry digest they were sealed with.

## Outstanding at retirement

This programme published commitments for target dates 2026-09-20 and 2026-09-21 before it
was retired. Those two days were committed before their gates and are settled after the
operating day, so their settled rows are not in the files above. They are owed, not
withdrawn, and are added to this directory once their settlement prices are available.
The commitments themselves are already public and sealed.
